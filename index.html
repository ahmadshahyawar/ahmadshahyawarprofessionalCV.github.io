Profile

Ahmad Shah Yawar is a Computer Science graduate who completed his Bachelor's degree at Al‑Farabi Kazakh National University with distinction, awarded the Red Diploma, and is now continuing as an MSc candidate at the same university. His academic work is centred on artificial intelligence and machine learning, with particular attention to computer vision problems such as automated crack detection in tunnel infrastructure and flood detection for early warning systems, alongside research into enterprise data warehousing for web-based business operations. This research background is paired with several years of independent, hands-on software development: designing, building, deploying, and personally maintaining complete software systems from the first line of code through to live production servers.

Across this practical work he has been responsible for the full lifecycle of each system — requirements and architecture, database design, full-stack implementation, and ongoing server and infrastructure administration — rather than a single narrow role within a larger team. That combination of academic rigor in AI/ML research and complete ownership of real, deployed software systems is the foundation he intends to bring into a professional IT Manager role, where structured thinking, technical depth across the stack, and accountability for systems end-to-end are equally essential.
